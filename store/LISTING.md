## Privacy answers

- **Apple App Privacy:** "Data Not Collected". The app has no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking; preferences stay on the device.
- **Google Play Data safety:** no data collected, no data shared; no account creation. Embedded YouTube videos load from YouTube only when the reader presses play (disclosed in the privacy policy).
- **Encryption / export compliance (Apple):** the app uses only standard HTTPS, so answer "No" to non-exempt encryption.

## Age rating

Answer the questionnaires honestly: no violence, no mature content, no user-generated content, no purchases. The app does contain **general health and medical information** (fitness, dementia, mental health), which both questionnaires ask about. Expect an everyone / low age rating.

## Notes for App Review (Apple)

> 4Everyone is a bilingual (English/Spanish) well-being magazine. No login is required. Tap the EN / ES switch at the top to change language. Highlights to try: Fitness > Start (2-minute guided movement break), Flavors > Pozole (recipe card), Check Your Knowledge (interactive crossword), Proverbs (audio clips), and the search button. Long videos stream from our YouTube channel; exercise videos, text, images and audio are built into the app and work offline.
